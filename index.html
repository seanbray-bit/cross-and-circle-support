Cross & Circle

Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Privacy Policy

Cross & Circle does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Player names, scores, game history, and achievements are saved only on your device using app storage.

Nearby Game uses Bluetooth and the local network only when you choose Host Game or Join Game. Multiplayer data is limited to connection details and game moves between nearby devices.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking domains, or third-party sign-in.
